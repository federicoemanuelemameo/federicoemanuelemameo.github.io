Abstract

Tubi is a playful installation that aims to make visitors reconnect with their inner child by playing with a colourful interactive playground structure.

Playing with its parts, visitors explore an archive of childhood memories: sequences of images generated from the childhood stories of real people. By embedding smartphones as sensors inside the wheel, the swing and the ball, the structure turns intuitive, tactile movement into real-time control over the story being told.

Interacting with Tubi

The challenge

Explore new forms of interaction by creating or modifying sensor-equipped objects that control digital environments and communicate in real time, enhancing user engagement with photographic archives displayed in museums or galleries.

Sketch

The solution

The installation transforms visitors into active participants, inviting them to play. The interaction is deliberately light-hearted and joyful, echoing the playful nature of the structure itself.

Each part of the playground controls one layer of the story: turning the wheel moves on to the next story, moving the swing brings the images out of the dark, moving the ball amplifies the sounds. The memories are not just observed, but explored and inhabited.

Ideally, the structure stands in the middle of a dark room, the only lit element. On the wall in front of it runs the image sequence of the story; on the side walls, the written story and a few general details about the person who lived it.

Structure

The structure was designed in Fusion 360: polypropylene tubes for the main frame, 3D-printed joints, caps, swing supports and wheel, and three smartphones used as wireless sensors for a cable-free setup. All the electronics are hidden to keep a clean, playful aesthetic.

Code and archive

The projection is a full-screen web interface built with HTML and JavaScript. It receives the motion data of the three smartphones via WebSocket and updates images and audio in real time.

The archive is made of childhood stories told by real people. From each story, a sequence of images generated with ComfyUI reconstructs the memory, frame by frame.
