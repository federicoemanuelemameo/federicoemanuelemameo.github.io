Abstract

Does being Swiss make you more likely to recycle in Lausanne? An interactive data visualization of the recycling habits of the city of Lausanne, Switzerland.

How does waste management vary across districts when comparing origin, the amount of waste produced, and efficiency in 2023?

The visualization in use

The visualization

The visualization was developed with D3.js, a JavaScript library for dynamic, data-driven graphics. Here it generates a circle-packing layout, scaling and positioning elements from hierarchical data: nested circles represent districts and waste categories, with animated transitions, scalable labels and interactive zooming that let users explore the dataset intuitively.

Overview

Key findings

While it's true that the areas with the most non-Swiss residents have the worst results, the areas with the most Swiss are not the clear leaders either. It would probably improve waste production and efficiency in Lausanne if the municipality engaged differently with non-Swiss residents.

At the same time, the districts with the best results are pretty evenly split between Swiss and non-Swiss (~55% Swiss), which indicates that other factors than origin are probably more relevant: age, access to infrastructure, or neighbourhood culture. Comparing age against these results would be the next demographic area to investigate.

By focusing on certain aspects of the data, it is easy to lead the viewer to one story that might not capture the full picture.
