Abstract

After 20 years of merging the digital and physical worlds, Random Studio offered a night of recalibration with four interactive installations during its 20th Anniversary Party.

Inter Facial is one of them: an interactive installation that acts as a digital mirror, but with a twist.

Inter Facial

The challenge

Design an interactive experience for Random Studio's 20th Anniversary that reflects the studio's legacy of merging digital and physical realms.

The goal was to design an installation based on the party theme: an uncanny wellness centre.

The invite

The solution

Using real-time face tracking and manipulation, Inter Facial swaps facial features between participants standing in front of it. This creates a surreal, hybrid identity that blends the physical presence of the users with a digital reconfiguration.

The installation invites guests to play with their own image and that of others, fostering connection and amusement, on large screens that immerse the participants in their digital reflection.

Research and concept

The initial phase involved researching facial recognition technologies and exploring the concept of digital identity, experimenting with different methods to find the most robust solution for real-time face tracking and manipulation.

A composed face

Prototyping

A first JavaScript prototype tested face tracking with MediaPipe. Then we explored ways of combining different face parts to create interesting compositions with one single face. In the end, we added the detection of a second face, combining it with the first to create more dynamic compositions.

For the final development the visual compositions were designed in Figma and then exported in code, matching the uncanny wellness theme of the event.
