Abstract

Goosto is a culturally-driven food discovery app powered by AI. It helps users explore new ethnic cuisines and restaurants through personalized recommendations and cultural storytelling.

For restaurants, it's a tool to share their heritage, connect with authenticity-seeking diners, and craft their narrative and menu with AI support. The platform also suggests engaging content to enhance visibility within the community.

Cover

The challenge

Envision and prototype a cognitive experience powered by Generative AI, designed to enhance user interactions within one of the following contexts: restaurant, cruises, airline, travel accommodation, travel experiences.

The solution

Goosto offers an integrated platform that combines cultural storytelling with personalized food discovery.

(a) AI-guided storybuilding: the agent helps the restaurant owner build their story and menu with a conversational interface.

(b) AI-powered storytelling: the agent guides the client to discover the story of the restaurant and the culture behind the dishes.

(c) Journey tracking: the user can see their journey through the cultures and cuisines they have tried, and receives personalized restaurant suggestions based on it.

Experience & interface design

After analysing the customer journey, we defined the user archetypes and the end-to-end experience, considering the features to implement and the AI agent's personality, as well as how it should interact with users.

The scope of the platform was a minimalistic design, with a focus on the cultural storytelling carried by the AI agents: vocal interaction and visual feedback give the user a better sense of immersion into the story. A set of guidelines for the design system covers colour palette, typography, icons and components.

Research

Since we were asked to design an AI agent for a cognitive experience, we began by exploring how AI tools are currently integrated into restaurant operations. Given the dual nature of the sector, we also focused on the client's perspective, analysing food discovery platforms and cultural storytelling applications.

While AI is increasingly present in the restaurant industry, its application is heavily weighted towards operational efficiency. Meanwhile, dining experiences are evolving towards a greater appreciation for diverse culinary traditions: the demand for authentic cultural immersion has gone beyond just the food.

Desk research

We spread a survey and conducted interviews with restaurant owners and ethnic food enthusiasts to understand their needs and pain points in discovering and sharing cultural food experiences.

Users are increasingly interested in authentic cultural experiences when dining out. Many participants expressed frustration with the lack of cultural context and storytelling in their dining experiences, highlighting an opportunity for the platform to bridge this gap.

Business model

During the project we also developed a business model and a marketing strategy. Small and medium-sized ethnic restaurants are the paying users: they benefit from reaching a wider audience interested in their cuisine and culture, and are increasingly willing to invest in digital services. Ethnic food lovers are free users.

Competition-based pricing orientation, penetration pricing strategy; the break-even point is calculated by dividing the fixed costs of the first year by the contribution margin of the subscriptions.
